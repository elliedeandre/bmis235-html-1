Laugh More

A page about how laughing more increases your life expectancy

It's scientifically proventhat laughing more lowers risk of all-cause mortality, heart disease, and infections while resetting your body.

A goodbelly laugh can change the state of your body. It can ease physical tensiona nd relax your muscles for up to 45 minutes by triggering a temporary increase in heart reate and oxygen intake followed by relaxation.

3 ways to laugh more

  • Spend time with funny friends and family, not people who drain your energy.
  • Adopt a playful mindset by looking for the irony and absurdity in daily frustrations.
  • Spend time with animals. They will make you feel warm and fuzzy inside.
